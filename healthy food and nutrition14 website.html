Nutritious Recipes

Fresh Vegetable Salad

A refreshing mix of colorful vegetables.

Calories: Approx. 180 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped vegetables
  • 1/2 lemon
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil

Preparation: Mix all ingredients in a bowl and serve fresh.

Healthy Breakfast Bowl

A nutritious breakfast with fruits and grains.

Calories: Approx. 300 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup oats
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 banana
  • A few nuts

Preparation: Cook oats with milk and add sliced banana and nuts.

Vegetable Soup

A warm soup made with vegetables and herbs.

Calories: Approx. 150 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 2 cups water
  • Garlic and pepper
  • Salt to taste

Preparation: Boil vegetables with water and seasoning until soft.

Healthy Eating Tips

💧 Drink enough water throughout the day.

🥗 Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.

🌾 Choose whole grains and fiber-rich foods.

🥚 Include protein-rich foods in your meals.

🍬 Limit excess sugar, salt and highly processed foods.

⏰ Maintain regular and balanced meal times.
